# agentme BDRs Index

Business decisions for software development project management practices in the agentme scope.

## Principles

Organisational structure, team topology, and strategic alignment concepts.

- [agentme-bdr-001](principles/001-company-organizational-levels.md) - **Company organizational levels** — Defines the Group, Company, and Business Unit taxonomy used to describe how organisations are structured
- [agentme-bdr-002](principles/002-okr-framework.md) - **OKR framework** — Defines the Strategic, Tactical, and Operational OKR framework and connection rules
- [agentme-bdr-003](principles/003-product-delivery-value-flow.md) - **Product delivery value flow** — Defines the end-to-end chain from Strategic OKRs through the eight lifecycle phases to fulfilled Tactical OKRs
- [agentme-bdr-004](principles/004-business-and-software-lifecycle.md) - **Business and software lifecycle** — Defines the eight canonical phases (Ideation to Operate, Improve and Retire), per-change application and the mapping to environments and test groups
- [agentme-bdr-005](principles/005-xdrs-single-source-of-truth.md) - **XDRS as the single source of truth** — XDRS hold everything about a solution except the codebase, written from the first brainstorm as if final and evolved in every phase

## Operations

Project management workflow and structure for development teams.

- [agentme-bdr-401](operations/401-plan-epic-feature-story.md) - **Epic / Feature / User Story planning** — Organize development work as epics, features, and user stories using XDRS initiative documents
- [agentme-bdr-402](operations/402-digital-product-roles.md) - **Digital product roles — organisation & cross-team** — Defines team purpose, workforce allocation, roles overview, and org-level and cross-team roles (PM, Principal Engineer, Solution Architect, Enterprise Architect, Business Owner)
- [agentme-bdr-403](operations/403-product-team-composition.md) - **Product team composition** — Defines team composition models, squad structure, squad dynamics, and known structural gaps for Product Teams
- [agentme-bdr-404](operations/404-team-roles-and-specialists.md) - **Digital product roles — team & specialists** — Defines team-level roles (PO, PjM, BA, AI BA, AI Lead, AI Engineer, Tech Lead, engineers) and shared specialist roles
- [agentme-bdr-405](operations/405-digital-product-roles-raci.md) - **Digital product roles — RACI** — RACI rules, role abbreviations and the team-operations rows; the lifecycle rows live in the phase policies
- [agentme-bdr-406](operations/406-gdpr-request-and-breach-workflow.md) - **GDPR request and breach workflow** — Roles, RACI, internal time targets, escalation, records and yearly drills for data subject requests and breaches
- [agentme-bdr-407](operations/407-phase-1-ideation.md) - **Phase 1 - Ideation** — Spikes, brainstorms, whiteboards, sandbox experiments and local prototypes; XDRS written from the first brainstorm
- [agentme-bdr-408](operations/408-phase-2-scope-requirements.md) - **Phase 2 - Scope Requirements** — Business plan, milestones, KPIs, objectives, team and owner assignment
- [agentme-bdr-409](operations/409-phase-3-risk-and-impact-assessment.md) - **Phase 3 - Risk and Impact Assessment** — CIA levels, AI system type, GDPR assessment, risk appetite, legal position and the risk classification record
- [agentme-bdr-410](operations/410-phase-4-architecture-security-and-control-design.md) - **Phase 4 - Architecture, Security and Control Design** — Systems, platforms, standards by risk class, control objectives turned into requirements, business controls and NFRs
- [agentme-bdr-411](operations/411-phase-5-develop-build-and-configure.md) - **Phase 5 - Develop, Build and Configure** — Implementation, configuration, control automation and development tests
- [agentme-bdr-412](operations/412-phase-6-validate-and-approve.md) - **Phase 6 - Validate and Approve** — Business and governance tests in acceptance, rollout strategy, operational readiness and go-live approval
- [agentme-bdr-413](operations/413-phase-7-deploy-and-rollout.md) - **Phase 7 - Deploy and Rollout** — Deployment to production, people support, health monitoring and rollout steps until handover to operations
- [agentme-bdr-414](operations/414-phase-8-operate-improve-and-retire.md) - **Phase 8 - Operate, Improve and Retire** — Monitoring, incidents, improvements, scheduled control checks, KPI reports and safe decommissioning
- [agentme-bdr-415](operations/415-solution-scope-lifecycle.md) - **Solution scope lifecycle** — Scope created at the first brainstorm, split per application, evolved through all phases and deleted after decommissioning
- [agentme-bdr-416](operations/416-solution-content-map.md) - **Solution content map** — Which XDRS document type and location holds each kind of information about a solution
- [analyse-cvs](operations/skills/analyse-cvs/SKILL.md) - **Analyse CVs** — Scores and ranks candidate CVs in a `.tmp/` folder against agreed weighted criteria for a role, with redaction, writing results to a run folder `.tmp/analyse-cvs/<id>/` with one folder and an interview chart per invited candidate *(skill)*
